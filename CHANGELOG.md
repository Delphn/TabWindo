# Changelog

Notable changes to TabWindo. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and
versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Switch windows, not apps, most recent first, with ⌘Tab, ⌥Tab or ⌃Tab.
- Windows on every display, on other Spaces and in full-screen apps, with minimized windows last.
- Live previews in a grid of 2 to 7 windows per row, on plates in each app's icon color.
- Calm plates in dark mode: a deeper shade of each app's color. The switcher follows the Mac between light and dark,
  including when it switches on its own.
- macOS gets its own ⌘Tab back whatever happens to TabWindo: quit, crash, or a hang. Nothing starts at the lock
  screen, and only one TabWindo runs at a time.
- Menu options to keep the list to the current desktop (other desktops and full-screen apps are shown by
  default) and to include the windows of hidden apps (left out by default).
- Tab, ⇧Tab or ⇧ alone, the arrow keys, Return, Esc and the mouse while the switcher is open.
- A menu bar icon, and launch at login.
