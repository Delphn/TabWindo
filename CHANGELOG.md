## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Switch windows, not apps, most recent first, with ⌘Tab, ⌥Tab or ⌃Tab.
- Windows on every display, on other Spaces and in full-screen apps, with minimized windows last.
- Live previews in a grid of 2 to 7 windows per row, on plates in each app's icon color.
- Tab, ⇧Tab or ⇧ alone, the arrow keys, Return, Esc and the mouse while the switcher is open.
- A menu bar icon, and launch at login.
